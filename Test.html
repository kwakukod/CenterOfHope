Mmarima Kuo
Men's Ministry
Iron Sharpens Iron

Brotherhood that doesn't let you fake it.

Mmarima Kuo is where the men of Center of Hope show up for each other — in prayer, in accountability, and in real life. We talk about marriage, fatherhood, work, temptation, and what it actually looks like to lead like Christ. No performances here, just brothers pushing each other toward Jesus.

  • Honest conversation, not small talk
  • Prayer & accountability partners
  • Teaching for real, everyday life
  • Service projects & cookouts

Rooted Together

Every season, fully known.

Mmaa Kuo is a place for the women of Center of Hope to be fully known — from new mothers to grandmothers, students to career women. We gather to pray, study the Word, and carry each other's burdens, because no woman here has to walk alone.

  • Deep, honest prayer
  • Bible study across generations
  • Mentorship — younger women & elders
  • Fellowship dinners & retreats
