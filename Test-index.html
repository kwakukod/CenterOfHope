02 Welcome Home

You're not visiting a service. You're joining a family.

Every gathering centers on worship, the Word, and a community that shows up for one another beyond Sunday.
"Whether it's your first Sunday or your five-hundredth, our doors and our hearts are open."

Come expecting to encounter God and to be known. Center of Hope is built on Spirit-filled worship, straightforward preaching, and real relationships that carry past the closing prayer.
